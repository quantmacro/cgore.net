Recycling policy is mostly written for household packaging, while much of the
plastic in circulation never passes through a household. This project surveys the
people who actually use and dispose of plastics outside that channel, starting
with row-crop and livestock operations in the American Midwest, and asks what
would have to change for more of that material to be recovered.
