Resilience investments are decided by people who disagree about how likely a
disaster is and how much a bad outcome would hurt. This project builds the
economic methods for that decision. It elicits risk preferences from individuals
and from groups. It also values outcomes that no market prices, and measures how
far expert risk judgments diverge from those of the communities experts advise.

Outputs include NIST guidance on nonmarket valuation, the Economic Decision Guide
software and its wildfire case studies, published survey instruments, and
empirical work on how past disasters shaped pandemic relief.
