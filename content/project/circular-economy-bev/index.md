Battery electric vehicles only deliver their promised benefits if buyers can judge
what they are getting, and battery condition is the hardest thing on the car to
judge. This project measures what buyers actually know about batteries and what
they will pay for better information, using discrete choice experiments and
national surveys.

The work runs from literature review through instrument design to estimation, and
the survey instruments are published so other researchers can reuse them.
